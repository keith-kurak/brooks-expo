## Scope and evidence

[observed] This note distinguishes US Brooks Run Club promotions from broader
Brooks-sponsored Strava challenges. Official rules establish membership,
eligibility, actions, prize quantities, and whether prizes are random. Amounts
below are the rules' approximate retail values (ARV), in USD. It is a sample of
documented campaigns, not a complete archive.

[observed] Prize descriptions below are offers in official rules, not evidence
that every advertised prize was claimed. Some retired PDFs return 404 today;
where stated, details were recovered from indexed text of the sponsor's PDF.
The rules often establish daily entry and random selection without preserving
the former game's animation, artwork or exact questions.

## Completed games and earlier promotions

### Memory Match, August–September 2021

[observed] August 1–September 28, 2021. US/DC adults could register and play
the memory-match instant-win game once daily. There were 1,000 randomly
timed winning plays, each offering four Nuun products and a water bottle,
ARV $35. Non-winners received a 30% Nuun discount, with exclusions and an
expiry. Winning the game did not establish a skill-based prize entitlement;
the rules selected winners by time and limited each person to one prize.
[Official rules, sections 3, 4, 6, 7](https://brooks.promo.eprize.com/running/public/COMPILED/en/fulfillment/rules.9dcb34f1604c2d0c273a4263c366de47.pdf).

### Trivia, January–April 2022

[observed] January 4–April 4, 2022. Answering questions provided one daily
instant-win play regardless of accuracy. There were 1,500 Honey Stinger
packages, each containing eight waffles, three energy-chew products and two
cracker bars, ARV $25.25. Non-winners received a 30% Honey Stinger promotion
code, with terms and an expiry. Winners were selected through randomly
generated winning times. These rules establish the trivia mechanic but not
the exact questions displayed.
[Official rules, sections 3, 4, 6, 7](https://brooks.promo.eprize.com/running/public/COMPILED/fulfillment/rules-phase_4.1e3e4409def2133abfcae62e24e1b66b.pdf).

### Training program and Marine Corps Marathon sweepstakes, April–June 2022

[observed] April 5–June 27, 2022. A daily registration/login entry fed a random
drawing. Choosing a training goal/program and marking activities complete
was optional, not required to enter. One offered trip for two to Washington,
DC included airfare, two hotel nights, $1,000 spending money, a $600 Brooks
eGift card and optional free Marine Corps Marathon participation, ARV
$6,950. There were also 201 prizes consisting of $25 Brooks offers.
[Official rules, sections 3–7](https://brooks.promo.eprize.com/running/public/COMPILED/fulfillment/rules-phase_5.01db517e4ad23d1d0e11a2f120e93a0b.pdf).

### Memory Match, January–March 2023

[observed] January 5–March 29, 2023. Registration/login led to one daily
memory-match instant-win play. The offer was 2,000 drawstring bags, ARV $8
each. Winning times were randomly generated; a maximum of one bag could
be awarded per person. The archived rules name the game but do not preserve
its visual implementation.
[Official rules, sections 3, 4, 6, 7](https://brooks.promo.eprize.com/running/public/fulfillment/rules-phase_8.pdf).

### Prize Wheel, June–September 2023

[observed] June 29–September 27, 2023. The title names a prize wheel; the
entry instructions also require answering questions, whose correctness
does not affect the daily play. One thousand randomly awarded Nuun packs
contained three tubes, two single-serving sachets and a zipper bag, ARV
$33.45 each. Non-winners received a 20% Nuun discount with terms and an
expiry. The rules do not establish a skill score or points balance.
[Official rules, sections 3, 4, 6, 7](https://brooks.promo.eprize.com/running/public/fulfillment/rules-phase_10.pdf).

### Run. Walk. Win. Rest., June–August 2024

[observed — indexed official rules; direct PDF returned 404] June 26–August
9, 2024. Tracked runs/walks and milestones earned entries. Examples included
a July Strava 5K and activity on ten days between July 26 and August 9.
A daily instant-win game offered 45 footwear codes worth $160 each. One
trip-for-two package included airfare, three hotel nights, $500 spending
money, $300 for a marathon entry and two shoe pairs, ARV $6,920. July 21
activity also entered a separate draw for ten Dairy Queen packages, each

[Official rules, sections 3, 4, 6, 9](https://brooks.promo.eprize.com/summer2024/public/fulfillment/rules.pdf).

[observed — indexed official rules; direct PDF returned 404] October

## Completed fitness and activity promotions

### Magic Milestone, October 2025

[observed] October 7–28, 2025; US/DC residents 18+, with Run Club membership.
Synced tracker milestones earned entries: one 5K run/walk, 25; at least six
miles over two consecutive days, 50; one 13.1-mile run/walk, 100. Maximum 175;
mail-in alternatives existed. Random drawing, not guaranteed rewards. Two
prize packages each offered a five-day/four-night Disney World vacation for two:
airfare where applicable, hotel, two January 11, 2026 marathon entries,
four-day Park Hopper tickets, $1,000, and two Brooks shoe pairs up to $200
each. ARV $9,304 per package, $18,608 total.
[Official rules, sections 1, 3–6](https://brooks.promo.eprize.com/milestonesweeps/public/fulfillment/rules.pdf).

### Glycerin 23 Challenge, February 2026

[observed — indexed official rules; direct PDF returned 404 during research]

one 10K, 50. Maximum 120, with mail-in alternatives. Random drawing around
March 16. Five grand prizes each included $500, Glycerin 23 shoes, hat,

### Sync Sweepstakes, February–June 2026

[observed] February 2–June 30, 2026; US/DC Run Club members 18+. Newly
syncing a device with DashLX earned ten entries; already-synced members
could use the mail-in method. Five monthly drawings, with non-winning
entries carrying forward. One hundred randomly awarded $30 Brooks eGift
codes per month, 500 total, ARV $15,000. The codes gave $30 off online
orders and expired December 31, 2026. Ten-entry and one-prize-per-person
limits. This rewarded device connection through a sweepstakes; it was not
a guaranteed signup credit.
[Official rules, Timing, How to Enter, Prizes](https://brooks.promo.eprize.com/syncsweeps2026/public/fulfillment/rules.pdf).

### Ghost Amp Challenge, August 2026

[observed] August 3–31, 2026; US/DC Run Club members 18+. Syncing and the
first run/walk each earned ten entries; a 5K, 30; starting before 9 a.m.,
20. During August 17–31, completing three different distance tiers
(0.01–3.99, 4–7.99, 8–11.99, 12+ miles) earned 50 grand-prize entries
and one shoe-drawing entry. Random prizes: two $8,000 packages each
containing $5,000 travel cash, $2,000 StubHub credit, $700 for two headphone
pairs, and two Ghost Amp pairs; plus 30 Ghost Amp pairs at $150. Maximum
120 grand-prize entries; mail-in alternative; total ARV $20,500.
[Official rules, Timing, How to Enter, Prizes](https://brooks.promo.eprize.com/summer2026/public/fulfillment/rules.pdf).

## Implication for the app pitch

[inferred] A convincing app demo can show tracker connection, a small set of
dated milestones, entry progress, prize details, and result/claim status.
Those mechanics extend documented US campaigns. Guaranteed rewards for
every completion, points balances, and competitive leaderboards would be
new proposals and should be labeled as such.

### The goal is JavaScript only

[confirmed — user direction, 2026-10-15] A change to the goal distance or the
challenge name must not need a new native build.

[observed] `WEEKLY_GOAL_METERS` in `src/features/challenges/model.ts` is the
only distance. `WEEKLY_GOAL` derives every label from it: the title
("Weekly 10K"), the badge text ("10K") and the distance ("10 km"). Screens,
route titles, accessibility labels and the widget read these labels.

[observed] `expo-widgets` receives the widget layout as a string from the app's
JavaScript at runtime, so the layout already updates with the bundle. The
layout cannot read module constants, so the store publishes `title`, `goal`
and `badge` in the widget props. The badge PNGs are frames only
(`assets/challenges/weekly-badge*.png`); the widget draws the badge text over
them in a `ZStack`, and the in-app `WeeklyBadge` draws it over SVG.

[observed] The only native string is the widget gallery name in
`app.config.ts`. It is now the goal-neutral "Weekly Challenge". Changing it
from "Weekly 5K" changed the native fingerprint once; later goal changes do not.

[observed] The widget copies each badge into the App Group only when the file
does not exist. A redrawn frame therefore needs a new file name, or upgraded
devices keep the old image.
