# Captured workout-goal flows

**Date:** 2026-10-13
**Agent:** Codex / GPT-6.1
**System:** Research
**Scope:** Provide additional Mobbin screenshots as complete app flow sequences.

## Outcome

[observed] Saved 27 full-resolution screenshots from six captured flows in
[`designs/run-club-flows/`](../designs/run-club-flows/), with original order,
canonical source links and screen captions. Published an interactive comparison
with flow selection and screenshot enlargement. No application or Paper design
changed.

## What worked well

[observed] Mobbin's flow search returned enrollment and progress sequences for
Nike Run Club, Strava and Peloton, plus Fitbit goal editing. A further search
found sharing from an actual completed Strava × Brooks challenge.

## Friction and blockers

[observed] Some app-specific searches returned other apps or unrelated flows:
Gymshark commerce account/search screens and Apple Health records. Those were
excluded after visual inspection. Curl downloaded all selected assets without
errors.

## What was hard

[observed] Screenshot counts include scrolling within a screen. Captions preserve
that distinction. The Alan collection flow shows a welcome gift, while its home
screen separately offers workout imports to earn rewards; the capture does not
show exercise completion causing the displayed gift.

## Comparative friction

Not observed. This task compared captured product designs rather than frameworks.

## Improvement ideas

[inferred] Mobbin flow search could distinguish enrollment confirmations from
completion celebrations, and return an explicit unavailable match when a named
app has only unrelated flows. Both would reduce manual filtering.
