# More Mobbin references for Run Club

**Date:** 2026-10-13
**Agent:** Codex / GPT-6.1
**System:** Research
**Scope:** Find additional screen inspiration for a simple workout-based goal.

## Outcome

[observed] Three Mobbin searches returned 12 screens. Selected seven after
visual inspection and saved their full-resolution images, canonical links and
notes in [`designs/run-club-inspiration/`](../designs/run-club-inspiration/).
Presented the selection in an interactive gallery. No app or Paper screen changed.

## What worked well

[observed] Separate queries for distance goals, weekly summaries and completion
states produced different useful patterns. Excluding earlier references surfaced
new screens, including another Brooks challenge screen from Strava.

## Friction and blockers

[observed] Python's urllib failed certificate validation with a missing local
issuer. Normal curl downloads succeeded with certificate verification enabled.

## What was hard

[observed] One completion result was an Any Distance supporter collectible, not
an earned workout milestone. Visual inspection caught the mismatch and it was
excluded from the shortlist.

## Comparative friction

Not observed. This task used reference search and image presentation.

## Improvement ideas

[inferred] Mobbin search could distinguish badges earned through activity from
collectibles granted for paid membership when searching for workout completion.
