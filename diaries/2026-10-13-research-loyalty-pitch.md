# Brooks mobile loyalty pitch

**Date:** 2026-10-13
**Agent:** Codex / gpt-6.1-sol
**System:** Research
**Scope:** Recommend loyalty concepts for an executive demo of the existing app.

## Outcome

[observed] LLP 0005, LLP 0006 and the Account screen establish an existing
Apple Health connection, shoe mileage, an iOS widget and local prototype
membership. The Account screen currently lists perks rather than challenge
progress or a reward wallet. No app code changed in this task.

[observed] Brooks already offers US member perks and a fitness-tracker milestone
sweepstakes on its [US Run Club page](https://www.brooksrunning.com/en_us/brooks-run-club/).
The [UK Run Club page](https://www.brooksrunning.com/en_gb/brooks-run-club/)
describes Strides, activity milestones and reward or community-contribution
choices. Regional rules differ; UK mechanics are not established US benefits.

[inferred] A first demo should connect a completed consistency challenge,
a chosen benefit and the existing shoe shopping journey. Member drops,
retailer event check-ins and a race journey are additional pitch candidates.
These are proposals, not approved Brooks program rules or confirmed uplift.

[observed] An inline HTML concept used illustrative activity and benefits.
T3 HTML previews at 728 and 360 pixels showed no horizontal overflow.
Scripted walkthroughs reached the demo bag through both reward and
community-contribution choices without console errors.

## What worked well

[observed] Existing LLPs identified reusable capabilities without starting a
native build. Primary Brooks pages distinguished existing programs from new
proposals. A small interactive sketch made the proposed demo path reviewable.

## Friction and blockers

[observed] An initial broad tool-catalog print produced oversized, truncated
output. Later reads restricted the catalog to relevant tool names.

## What was hard

[inferred] The app pitch needs to explain the value of repeat use while keeping
new rewards distinct from Brooks' existing regional programs. The sketch marks
its fixtures as illustrative.
